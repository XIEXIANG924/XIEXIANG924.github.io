About

I am a first-year M.Sc. student in Geoinformatics Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, with a B.Eng. in Transportation Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of geospatial data science, multi-source IoT data fusion, and autonomous systems — building structured, trustworthy information layers that machines can rely on.

I am the first author of the Location Prior Generation Framework (LPGF), a quality-gated data fusion pipeline for low-altitude air mobility, currently under review at IEEE TITS. I have also led a national-level research project on urban travel behavior analysis (559K POI records), built a full-stack WebGIS platform for air quality monitoring in Portugal, and hold three invention patents.

I am an Erasmus+ scholarship recipient, an avid open-source contributor, and a multi-genre gamer who thinks about system design even when off the clock.

Selected Projects

LPGF — Location Prior Generation Framework
Xiang Xie, Xiaonan Liu · IEEE TITS (Under Review)
A quality-gated multi-source data fusion pipeline that integrates Sentinel-2 imagery, UAV telemetry, vehicle GPS trajectories, and OSM building footprints into structured, reusable 3D location priors for the low-altitude economy. The two-stage four-criterion quality gate ensures controlled degradation under adverse imaging conditions, with a three-tier fallback bounded at 5.5m error.
Python Sentinel-2 OSM Quality Gate LPSP-v0 QGIS
Portugal Air Quality WebGIS
Politecnico di Milano GIS Lab · GIS Data Analyst & Tech Lead
Full-stack WebGIS platform for spatial analysis of PM₁₀, NO₂, and PM₂.₅ across Portugal (2021–2023). Built on CAMS NetCDF reanalysis data (835MB, 744 bands), ESRI 10m land cover, and WorldPop population data. Features a 13-layer GeoServer WMS backend and an Apple-style OpenLayers frontend with Chart.js visualizations.
